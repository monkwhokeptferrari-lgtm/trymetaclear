Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remove the Gemini watermark from an image?

Drag and drop your image (PNG, JPG or WEBP) into the upload box. The tool automatically finds the visible Gemini watermark in the bottom-right corner and fills it in using the surrounding pixels. Compare the Result and Original tabs, check the corner close-up, then click Download clean image.

Does this remove SynthID or other invisible watermarks?

No. This tool removes only the visible watermark shown in the corner of the image. It does not remove invisible watermarks embedded directly into the pixels, such as Google DeepMind SynthID, because those are structural pixel patterns rather than a visible overlay.

Will removing the watermark reduce my image quality or resolution?

No. Your image keeps its original resolution. Only the small area covered by the watermark is rebuilt from the neighbouring pixels, and every other pixel stays exactly the same. Export as PNG for a fully lossless result, or choose JPEG for a smaller file.

Are my images uploaded or stored on your server?

No. Everything happens inside your browser, on your own device. Your image is never uploaded, stored or shared, and you do not need an account.

What if the watermark is not detected automatically?

Raise the Sensitivity slider, which helps with faint watermarks on light backgrounds. You can also switch on the manual brush and paint over the watermark, and it is filled in instantly. Results look best on smooth or gradient backgrounds; on busy textures, check the corner close-up before downloading.

Does the downloaded file keep EXIF or C2PA metadata?

No. The cleaned image is saved as a fresh file built from the pixels, so embedded metadata such as EXIF, XMP and C2PA Content Credentials is not carried over. For a full metadata scan and dedicated cleaning, use the TryMetaClear AI Metadata Remover.

Which image formats and file sizes are supported?

You can upload PNG, JPG and WEBP images up to 40 MB or 40 megapixels. The cleaned image can be downloaded as a PNG (lossless) or a JPEG.

Is the Gemini watermark remover free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no sign-up, no limit on the number of images, and nothing added to your result. Please use it only on images you created or have permission to edit.
