Frequently Asked Questions (Video Metadata)

What types of video files are supported?

Our tool supports MP4, QuickTime (MOV), and WebM video formats up to 100MB, scanning both standard container metadata and AI generator tags.

Does this tool re-encode or compress my video?

No. TryMetaClear cleans container atoms directly without altering your video or audio bitstreams, meaning zero quality loss and instant processing speed.

Can it remove invisible pixel watermarks like SynthID?

No. This tool successfully removes metadata headers, text tags, prompt histories, and manifest atoms, but it does not alter invisible pixel-level neural watermarks.
