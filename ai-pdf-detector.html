    What the AI PDF Detector checks

    • C2PA Content Credentials: signed provenance records attached to the PDF, and whether they explicitly declare generative AI.
    • Hidden XMP data: AI markers such as the IPTC digital source type trainedAlgorithmicMedia, which apps write when content is made with generative AI.
    • Creator and Producer fields: whether the software that made the PDF names an AI tool such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot.
    • Images inside the PDF: AI labels in photos and illustrations, such as C2PA from DALL-E or Firefly, or IPTC "AI generated" fields.

    Why most AI-written PDFs show "No AI signals"

    When you ask an AI assistant for a PDF, it usually writes the content as a web page or document and converts it with ordinary software, such as a headless Chrome browser on a server. That software records itself as the creator. It doesn't add an AI label, and neither does the assistant.

    So a PDF with no AI signals may still have been written with AI. The detector tells you what the file says, honestly. It doesn't guess from the writing style, because AI-writing detectors are unreliable and regularly flag text written by people.

    AI PDF Detector frequently asked questions

    How does the AI PDF Detector work?

    It reads the PDF's file data in your browser: the creator and producer fields, the hidden XMP metadata, any C2PA Content Credentials, and the metadata of images inside the PDF. It reports every AI signal it finds, with the evidence.

    Can it tell if the text was written by ChatGPT or another AI?

    Only if the file says so. It doesn't judge writing style, because AI-writing detectors are unreliable and often flag text written by people. Most AI assistants don't label the PDFs they export.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. The detector runs entirely in your browser, so the PDF never leaves your device.

    What does "No AI signals in the file" mean?

    Nothing in the PDF's data says AI was used. It doesn't prove a person wrote it, because AI tools rarely label PDFs and metadata is easy to remove.

    Which AI tools can it identify?

    Any tool that names itself in the file or writes standard AI markers. It recognises names such as ChatGPT, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion and Adobe Firefly, as well as the IPTC AI source type and C2PA statements that declare AI.

    Does it work on scanned PDFs?

    Yes. It reads the file data of any PDF, including scans. Scanning apps rarely add AI labels, so scanned documents usually show no AI signals.

    How do I remove AI labels and other metadata from a PDF?

    Use the free PDF Metadata Remover. It removes C2PA Content Credentials, XMP data, the author, software, dates and other hidden details, also in your browser.
