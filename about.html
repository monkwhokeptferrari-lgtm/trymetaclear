About TryMetaClear

TryMetaClear is a modern privacy and metadata analysis platform engineered by Mayank Gupta for digital creators, marketers, photographers, and web users.

As generative AI tools and media authenticity standards like C2PA become widespread, understanding what hidden information resides inside your image files is essential. TryMetaClear was built to provide fast, header-level inspection and removal of EXIF, IPTC, XMP, GPS location tags, and C2PA Content Credentials manifests—allowing you to sanitize your media without altering or degrading original image quality.
