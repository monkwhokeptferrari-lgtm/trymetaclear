Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. File Upload & Processing Security

Your privacy is our primary engineering focus. All file transfers between your browser and our backend occur over encrypted HTTPS connections. Uploaded image files are processed strictly in isolated temporary server memory buffers.

2. Temporary File Retention

We do not store, archive, or retain your uploaded or processed media. Temporary files created during metadata scanning or cleaning operations are permanently deleted immediately after your cleaned image payload is generated.

3. Analytics & Cookies

We utilize Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Microsoft Clarity to understand aggregate web traffic and improve UI usability. These services collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic information such as browser type, device category, and page views.
