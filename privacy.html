Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

At TryMetaClear, accessible from https://trymetaclear.com, your privacy is a top priority. This Privacy Policy document outlines how we handle data when you use our image and video AI metadata removal tools.

2. File Processing & Temporary Storage

When you upload images (PNG, JPG, WebM) or videos (MP4, MOV, WebM) through our scanning and cleaning endpoints, files are processed temporarily in server memory solely to extract metadata atoms and strip tracking tags. Files are never permanently stored, shared, or indexed and are automatically purged immediately after the cleaned file is generated and downloaded.

3. Log Files & Analytics

Like standard web services, we utilize server logs and analytics tools (such as Microsoft Clarity and Google Analytics 4) to track aggregate site performance, browser types, and general traffic data to improve user experience.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our privacy practices, please contact us via our contact page.
